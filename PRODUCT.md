## Product purpose

Personal portfolio for Timmy Ajakaiye, a product designer and UI/UX engineer with a mechatronics engineering background. The site exists to win interviews and freelance work: a hiring design lead or founder should land here, feel the craft within ten seconds, and read at least one case study.

## Users

- Design leads and recruiters at fintech and sustainability companies, skimming dozens of portfolios in an afternoon.
- Founders looking for a designer who can own a product end to end (research through handoff, plus front-end fluency).

## Brand voice

Machined, confident, quietly warm. An engineer's precision applied to human problems. The voice of a CNC-milled nameplate, not a moodboard.

## Strategic principles

- The case study images are the merchandise. The site is the gallery wall: warm, calm paper that makes colorful app screens pop.
- Engineering heritage is the differentiator. Indices, coordinates, mono labels, and measured motion say "this person builds systems," without cosplay.
- One committed accent (blueprint orange) carries every interactive state. Nothing else competes with the work.
- Motion is choreography, not decoration: one orchestrated load, scroll-driven reveals, exponential ease-outs only.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-mode developer portfolio with neon gradients and glassmorphism.
- Template-grade Dribbble portfolio: identical card grids, hero metric counters, gradient text.
- Editorial-magazine costume (drop caps, broadsheet rules) on a brief that is engineering-flavored, not literary.
