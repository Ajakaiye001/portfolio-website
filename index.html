About — 01

Engineer turned designer. Still building things.

I came to design through systems rather than aesthetics. My background in software design and cybersecurity taught me that every part exists in relation to the whole, and that a product tends to fail at its weakest joint, which is usually the interface.

Most of my work sits in B2B and SaaS products across fintech and sustainability, where trust and clarity aren't nice-to-haves. They're the product. I'll take a project from discovery all the way through handoff, whether I'm working solo or embedded in a team.
