Kora

A financial analytics platform that transformed complex data into clear, confident decisions — cutting onboarding time by 60%.

Platform Web App

Year 2023

Kora is a B2B financial analytics dashboard designed for growing businesses that need clarity across revenue, churn, and conversion — without requiring a data team to interpret it. The design challenge was building trust through simplicity in a domain where complexity is expected.
