About

Design is the gap between
intention and experience.

I'm Timmy — a product designer and UI/UX engineer who believes the best interfaces are the ones users never have to think about. Raised on the energy of Lagos, sharpened in London.

With 5+ years shaping digital products across fintech, health, and consumer apps, I bring together sharp visual thinking and deep user empathy. I don't just design screens — I design the feeling of using them.

"Every pixel is a decision. Every interaction is a promise. I design both with intention."
