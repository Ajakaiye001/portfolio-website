About — 01

Engineer turned designer. Still building things.

I came to design through systems, not aesthetics. Mechatronics taught me that every part exists in relation to the whole, and that a product fails at its weakest joint, which is usually the interface.

Most of my work lives in fintech and sustainability, where trust and clarity aren't nice-to-haves. They're the product. I take projects from discovery through handoff, solo or embedded in teams.
