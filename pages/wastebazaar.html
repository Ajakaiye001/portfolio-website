Case Study — Mobile App

Waste
Bazaar

An on-demand recycling platform connecting households with verified collection drivers — making sustainable habits effortless.

Role Product Designer
Platform Mobile App
Focus UX / UI · Sustainability · Logistics
Year 2024
01 — Overview

Recycling,
reimagined.

WasteBazaar is a mobile platform that simplifies recycling by allowing users to schedule pickups or drop-offs for recyclable waste. It connects households with verified recycling drivers while a GreenCredits reward system encourages sustainable behaviour.

UX Research Ideation UI Design Prototype
02
02 — Problem Statement

Recycling is broken
by design.

Despite growing awareness of sustainability, the infrastructure to act on it remains fragmented, unreliable, and unincentivised.

  • Users don't know where or how to recycle
  • Pickup services are unreliable and unverified
  • No reward system for sustainable habits
  • Poor coordination between users and collectors

05 — User Insights & Interview

Four principles that shaped every decision.

Convenience drives adoption

If scheduling takes more than two taps, users abandon it. Frictionless booking is the foundation.

Incentives increase engagement

GreenCredits turns recycling from obligation into a rewarding habit loop users return to.

Trust systems are essential

Verified driver profiles and QR confirmation turn strangers into trusted partners.

Simplicity improves usability

Every extra screen is a drop-off point. The flow was reduced to its minimum viable steps.

06
06 — User Flow

Five steps. Zero friction.

The core journey was reduced to its minimum viable steps: Book → Confirm → Pickup → Verify → Reward.

07
07 — UX Decisions

Every decision, justified.

Key interaction decisions were documented and validated through usability testing. Each choice maps directly to a user insight or research finding.

  • 3-step booking flow — reduced from 6 steps in v1
  • Driver card shown before confirmation — builds trust early
  • GreenCredits progress always visible — motivates completion
  • QR scan as primary verification — faster than manual input
08
08 — Wireframes

Structure before style.

Low-fidelity layouts mapped the core flows — scheduling, driver matching, and rewards — before any visual design began. Tested with 8 users to validate mental models and information hierarchy.

09
09 — QR Code Pickup Verification

Trust,
by design.

Every pickup is verified through a unique QR code generated at booking and scanned at handoff. This creates a tamper-proof confirmation record — visible to both user and driver.

  • Unique QR generated per booking
  • Scanned by driver on arrival — no manual confirmation needed
  • Instant GreenCredits credit on successful scan
  • Dispute resolution supported by scan timestamp

13 — Reflection

What this taught me.

  • 01

    Simplicity in user flows is not a constraint — it's the design. Every step removed is a user retained.

  • 02

    Incentives change behaviour, but only if they feel proportional and visible. Progress must be shown, not assumed.

  • 03

    In logistics platforms, trust is the product. Verification and transparency aren't features — they're the foundation.

"The best recycling experience is the one users never have to think about."
