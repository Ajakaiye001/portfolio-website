The Problem

Understanding the Problem

A breakdown of why meaningful school connections fade over time.

As students graduate and move into different phases of life, the natural connections formed during school begin to fade. Distance, career paths, and evolving priorities make it difficult to maintain those relationships.

Fragmented Connections

Alumni gradually lose touch over time

  • Geographic distance
  • Changing lifestyles
  • Reduced communication

No Dedicated Platform

No platform built for school-based reconnection

  • Social media is too broad
  • No focus on shared history
  • Weak community structure

Event Coordination Gaps

Reunions are difficult to organise

  • No central hub
  • Scattered communication
  • Low participation

Missed Opportunities

Valuable connections go unused

  • No mentorship pathways
  • Limited networking
  • Shared experiences fade

Loss of Identity

Community identity weakens over time

  • Memories aren't preserved
  • Emotional connection fades
  • School culture is lost
Key Insight

People don't just lose contact — they lose access to a shared identity, network, and history that could continue to add value throughout their lives.

This revealed an opportunity to design a platform that reconnects people through shared history, structured communities, and meaningful interaction.

"Nostalgia isn't sentiment —
it's a signal. One that most platforms ignore."

The Vision

Three intentions.
Every decision traced back to one of them.

Reconnect

Make it effortless to find and re-enter the communities that shaped you — schools, sports teams, workplaces, old neighbourhoods.

Remember

Give every community a living archive — photos, milestones, and the stories in between. Built to outlast any platform.

The Identity

Brand Identity —

Concept

Quotation marks are universally recognised as symbols of speech — representing words, voices, and shared expression. For Yearbook, this symbolism becomes central. The platform is built around capturing memories, stories, and interactions between people who once shared a common space. The quotation mark becomes a visual metaphor for those voices — past and present.

Rationale

  • Represents individual voices within a collective
  • Reflects shared stories and memories
  • Reinforces the idea of conversation and reconnection
  • Symbolises a living archive of experiences

Rather than a static identity, the logo embodies an ongoing dialogue between people.

Reflection

Three things this
project taught me.

01
Simplicity improves usability.

Every screen removed is a barrier eliminated. The best interfaces feel inevitable, not engineered — they exist because they had to, not because someone put them there.

02
Familiar patterns lower activation energy.

When something feels known, users trust it faster — and engage deeper. The art is using familiarity as a foundation, then doing something unexpected on top of it.

03
Emotion-driven design is the most powerful design.

When an interface connects to memory and identity, it transcends utility. It becomes something people return to not because they need to — but because they want to.

"The best memory platform is the one that makes you feel
less alone in your past."
